# SageMind AI

> Research. Verify. Compare.

SageMind AI is a production-ready Multi-Agent Investment Research Platform designed to help investors perform explainable, evidence-based stock research using AI.

---

## 🚀 Planned Features

- 🤖 Multi-Agent AI Research
- 📈 Financial Analysis
- 📰 News Intelligence
- 🌍 Social Sentiment Analysis
- 📊 Portfolio Management
- ⭐ Watchlists
- 🔥 Trending Stocks
- 📑 AI Research Reports
- 📂 Sector Explorer
- 📉 Company Comparison

---
